# Week 2

Week 2 focuses on the cognitive side of HCI: how people perceive information, pay attention, remember, learn, solve problems, and form expectations about interactive systems.

- [Problem Solving and Decision Making](problem-solving-decision-making/)
- [Mental Models](mental-models/)
- [Gulfs of Execution and Evaluation](gulfs-execution-evaluation/)
- [Distributed Cognition](distributed-cognition/)

## Learning Focus

By the end of this week, you should be able to connect interface design decisions to human cognitive limits and explain how concepts such as attention, memory, mental models, and cognition in context affect usability.
