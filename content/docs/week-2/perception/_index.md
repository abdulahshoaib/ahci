# Perception

Perception is how people organize and interpret sensory information. Users do not simply see pixels or hear sounds; they interpret patterns, relationships, meanings, and possible actions.

## Visual Perception

Designers use grouping, contrast, proximity, alignment, and hierarchy to help users understand structure quickly.

## Auditory and Tactile Perception

Sound and touch can communicate alerts, confirmation, rhythm, urgency, or physical state, but they must be used carefully because context affects interpretation.

## Design Implication

Interfaces should make important differences easy to perceive and should avoid relying on subtle distinctions that some users may miss.
