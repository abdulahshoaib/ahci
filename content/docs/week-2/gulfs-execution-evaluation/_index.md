# Gulfs of Execution and Evaluation

The gulf of execution and the gulf of evaluation describe gaps between a user's goals and the system's interface.

## Gulf of Execution

The gulf of execution is the gap between what the user wants to do and the actions the system makes available. This gulf is large when users cannot find or understand the correct action.

## Gulf of Evaluation

The gulf of evaluation is the gap between the system's state and the user's ability to understand what happened. This gulf is large when feedback is unclear, delayed, or missing.

## Design Implication

Good design narrows both gulfs by making actions visible and making system state easy to interpret.
