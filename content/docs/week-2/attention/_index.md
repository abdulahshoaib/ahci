# Attention

Attention is the limited mental resource people use to focus on selected information while ignoring other information. Since users cannot attend to everything at once, interface design must guide attention carefully.

## Design Considerations

- Place important information where users are likely to look.
- Use contrast, size, motion, or spacing to emphasize priority.
- Avoid unnecessary alerts, clutter, and competing visual elements.
- Support divided attention in tasks such as driving, monitoring, or multitasking.

## Example

A clear error message next to the field that caused the problem directs attention better than a generic message at the top of a long form.
