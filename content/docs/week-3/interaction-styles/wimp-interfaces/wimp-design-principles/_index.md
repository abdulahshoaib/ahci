# WIMP Design Principles

WIMP interfaces rely on visual clarity, predictable behavior, and direct manipulation. Their design should make objects and actions understandable at a glance.

## Principles

- Keep important controls visible.
- Use consistent icons, menus, and window behavior.
- Provide immediate feedback for selection and action.
- Support undo or recovery where possible.
- Avoid overcrowding windows and toolbars.
- Use familiar conventions for common tasks.

## Design Implication

A good WIMP interface lets users explore, act, and recover without needing to memorize hidden command syntax.
