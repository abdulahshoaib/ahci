# Form-Fill Interfaces

Form-fill interfaces collect structured information through fields, labels, controls, and validation. They are common in registration, checkout, surveys, and administrative systems.

## Strengths

- Familiar and predictable.
- Good for structured data entry.
- Supports validation and constraints.

## Weaknesses

- Long forms can feel tedious.
- Poor labels or validation messages cause errors.
- Required formats may be unclear without examples.

## Design Implication

Forms should group related fields, use clear labels, mark required information, validate helpfully, and preserve user input after errors.
