# Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets combine data entry, calculation, organization, and visualization in a grid-based interface. They are powerful because users can build models directly by manipulating cells and formulas.

## Strengths

- Flexible and familiar for many users.
- Supports calculation and comparison.
- Makes data relationships visible in rows and columns.
- Allows incremental construction and experimentation.

## Weaknesses

- Complex formulas can be hard to audit.
- Errors may spread across dependent cells.
- Large sheets can become difficult to understand.
