# Reliability & Validity

Reliability means evaluation gives consistent results. Validity means evaluation measures what it claims to measure.

## Reliability

Same method under similar conditions should produce similar findings.

## Validity

Tasks, users, metrics, and setting should match real design question.

## Design Implication

Unreliable or invalid evaluation can lead to confident but wrong design decisions.
