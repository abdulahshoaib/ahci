# Iterative Evaluation

Iterative evaluation repeats design, test, and revision cycles.

## Cycle

1. Prototype or implement.
2. Evaluate.
3. Identify problems.
4. Redesign.
5. Evaluate again.

## Design Implication

Small repeated evaluations usually improve design more than one late evaluation after major decisions are fixed.
