# Field Studies

Field Studies are empirical evaluation methods in which users are observed in their natural environment while performing real activities.

The purpose is to understand how people use a system in real-world contexts, where factors such as surroundings, interruptions, and social interactions can influence behavior.

Unlike laboratory testing, field studies involve little control over what users do, allowing researchers to observe authentic usage patterns.

## Example

Observing people using Google Maps while:

- Driving
- Walking
- Navigating unfamiliar locations

## Where Applied

- Homes
- Offices
- Streets
- Public spaces
- Real-world environments

## When to Use

Field studies are most useful when:

- Context of use is important
- Real-world behavior needs to be understood
- Environmental factors may affect interaction

## Characteristics

- Real users
- Natural environment
- Low researcher control
- Focus on actual behavior and context
