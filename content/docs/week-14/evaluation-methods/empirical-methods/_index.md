# Empirical Methods

Empirical methods gather evidence from users, behavior, or real-world use.

## Examples

- Usability testing.
- Field studies.
- A/B testing.
- Surveys.
- Analytics.

## Design Implication

Empirical methods reveal what users actually do, not only what designers predict.
