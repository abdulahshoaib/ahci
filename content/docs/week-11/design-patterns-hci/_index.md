# Design Patterns in HCI

Design patterns are reusable solutions to common interaction problems. They capture problem, context, solution, and tradeoffs.

## Examples

- Search with filters.
- Breadcrumb navigation.
- Progressive disclosure.
- Inline validation.
- Undo.

## Design Implication

Patterns speed design and improve consistency, but they must fit user task and context.
