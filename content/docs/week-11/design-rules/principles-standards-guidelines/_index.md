# Principles, Standards, and Guidelines

Principles are broad design ideas. Standards are formal rules. Guidelines are practical recommendations for consistent design.

## Differences

- **Principles:** general, flexible, high-level.
- **Standards:** specific, required, often official.
- **Guidelines:** practical advice, often local to platform or organization.

## Design Implication

Principles help reason. Standards ensure compliance. Guidelines help teams build consistent interfaces faster.
