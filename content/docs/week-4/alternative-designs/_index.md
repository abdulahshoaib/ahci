# Alternative Designs

Alternative designs are different possible solutions to the same problem. Exploring alternatives prevents teams from committing too early to the first idea.

## Why Alternatives Help

- Reveal tradeoffs between solutions.
- Encourage creativity.
- Reduce attachment to one design.
- Support comparison through sketches, prototypes, or scenarios.

## Design Implication

Teams should generate and compare multiple designs before selecting one direction, especially for important workflows or high-risk usability problems.
