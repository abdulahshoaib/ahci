# Problem Space

The problem space defines what problem is being addressed, who experiences it, why it matters, and what constraints shape possible solutions. Clear problem framing prevents designers from solving the wrong problem.

## Key Questions

- What is the current situation?
- What problems or opportunities exist?
- Who is affected?
- What goals must the design support?
- What constraints limit the solution?

## Design Implication

A well-defined problem space guides design decisions and helps teams avoid jumping too quickly to interface details.
