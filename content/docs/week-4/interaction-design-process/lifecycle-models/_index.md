# Lifecycle Models

Lifecycle models describe how design and development activities are organized over time. In HCI, lifecycle models help teams plan research, prototyping, implementation, and evaluation.

## Examples

- Waterfall model.
- Spiral model.
- Star lifecycle model.
- Usability engineering lifecycle.
- Agile and iterative models.

## Design Implication

Lifecycle models should leave room for user feedback and design iteration, because usability problems often appear only after people interact with prototypes or working systems.
