# Interaction Design Activities

Interaction Design consists of four basic activities that are carried out throughout the design process. These activities are closely connected and often repeated in an iterative cycle.

## Discovering Requirements

The first activity is to identify and understand the requirements for the product.

This involves:

- Understanding users
- Understanding user goals
- Understanding user tasks
- Understanding the environment in which the product will be used

The information gathered forms the foundation for later design decisions.

## Designing Alternatives

Once requirements are understood, different design solutions are generated.

This activity focuses on:

- Exploring multiple ideas
- Creating different ways of meeting user needs
- Considering alternative approaches rather than selecting the first solution

Generating alternatives is important because it helps identify better and more effective designs.

## Prototyping Alternative Designs

Alternative designs are represented through prototypes.

Prototypes allow designers to:

- Visualize ideas
- Demonstrate functionality
- Explore design concepts
- Test design solutions before full development

Prototypes can range from simple sketches to more detailed interactive versions.

## Evaluating the Product and User Experience

Evaluation is carried out throughout the design process.

The purpose is to determine:

- Whether user requirements are being met
- Whether the product is usable
- Whether the desired user experience is achieved

Evaluation provides feedback that helps identify problems and areas for improvement.

## Relationship Between the Activities

The four activities are not performed only once in a fixed sequence. Findings from evaluation may lead to new requirements, new design alternatives, or changes to existing prototypes.

The activities therefore form an iterative process:

1. Discover requirements
2. Design alternatives
3. Prototype designs
4. Evaluate designs
5. Refine and repeat as needed

This continuous cycle helps ensure that the final product meets user needs and provides a positive user experience.
