# Users and Stakeholders

Users are the people who directly interact with a system. Stakeholders are all people or groups affected by the system, including users, clients, managers, support staff, administrators, and organizations.

## Why They Matter

- Different groups may have different goals.
- Power users and beginners may need different support.
- Stakeholders may define constraints, priorities, or success measures.
- Ignoring one group can create adoption or usability problems.

## Design Implication

Designers must identify primary users, secondary users, and wider stakeholders before defining requirements or evaluating success.
