# User Needs

Understanding user needs is a fundamental part of interaction design. Designers must determine what users are trying to achieve and how technology can help improve their activities and experiences.

Identifying user needs is not simply a matter of asking users what they want. Users often do not know what is technologically possible and may find it difficult to express requirements for new systems.

## Users Rarely Know What Is Possible

Designers should avoid relying solely on stakeholders to define requirements because users may be unaware of potential solutions and innovations.

Instead of expecting users to provide complete requirements, designers should investigate the situation more deeply.

## Exploring User Needs

To understand user needs, designers should:

- Explore the problem space
- Investigate who the users are
- Investigate users' activities
- Examine what aspects of current activities can be improved
- Try out ideas with potential users

These activities help reveal underlying needs that users may not explicitly state.

## Focus on Goals Rather Than Solutions

The emphasis should be on understanding what people want to achieve rather than asking them to design the system.

Designers should focus on:

- People's goals
- Usability goals
- User experience goals

By understanding goals, designers can create solutions that better support users' tasks and expectations.

## Improving Existing Activities

Studying how users currently perform their activities helps identify:

- Problems in existing systems
- Inefficiencies in workflows
- Opportunities for improvement
- Areas where technology can provide better support

This understanding guides the development of more effective and meaningful designs.

## Role in Interaction Design

Understanding user needs provides a foundation for:

- Discovering requirements
- Designing alternatives
- Building prototypes
- Evaluating solutions

Accurate understanding of user needs increases the likelihood that the final product will be useful, usable, and capable of providing a positive user experience.
