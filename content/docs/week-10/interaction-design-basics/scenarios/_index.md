# Scenarios

Scenarios are stories that describe how users interact with a system to achieve a goal. They help designers understand, communicate, and evaluate design ideas from the user's perspective.

Scenarios focus on:

- What users want to do
- What they see
- What actions they perform
- What they are thinking during the interaction

A scenario typically provides a step-by-step walkthrough of an activity, showing how a user moves through a system to complete a task.

Scenarios are used to:

- Communicate ideas to designers, clients, and users
- Validate designs and other models
- Understand user behaviour and system dynamics
- Explore interactions and user thinking
- Reuse design knowledge throughout development

## Areas Explored by Scenarios

### Interaction

- What actions users perform
- What happens after each action
- How the system responds

### Cognition

- What users are thinking
- What decisions they make
- What information they need

### Architecture

- What processes occur behind the scenes
- How the system supports the interaction

## Characteristics of Scenarios

Scenarios are linear because they describe one path through a system.

### Advantages

- Easy to understand
- Match the way people naturally think in stories
- Concrete and realistic
- Less likely to overlook important details

### Limitations

- Show only one path
- Do not represent all choices and alternatives
- May miss unusual situations and errors

Because of these limitations, designers should create multiple scenarios and combine them with other design methods.

## Example

Brian wants to watch a new movie and decide whether a friend would enjoy it. He downloads the movie to his computer, connects a portable movie player using Bluetooth, transfers the movie, and monitors the download progress on the device.

This scenario helps designers understand:

- User goals
- User actions
- Device interactions
- System feedback
- Information required at each step

Scenarios are used throughout the design process to explore, communicate, evaluate, and refine design ideas.
