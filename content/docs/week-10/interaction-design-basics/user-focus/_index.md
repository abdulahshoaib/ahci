# User Focus

User focus means design choices are based on target users, their goals, abilities, environments, and constraints.

## Questions

- Who will use system?
- What do they need to achieve?
- What knowledge do they already have?
- What errors or difficulties are likely?
- What context affects use?

## Design Implication

Design that ignores users may still be functional, but likely becomes hard to learn, inefficient, or unsuitable in real context.
