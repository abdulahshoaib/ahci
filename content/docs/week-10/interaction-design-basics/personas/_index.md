# Personas

Personas are evidence-based user profiles that help teams keep specific users in mind during design.

## Contents

- Name and role.
- Goals.
- Skills and knowledge.
- Needs and frustrations.
- Context of use.

## Design Implication

Personas help prevent vague "average user" thinking. They should be grounded in research, not stereotypes.
