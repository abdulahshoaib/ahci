# Localisation

Localisation adapts interface for language, culture, region, and local conventions.

## Concerns

- Translated text length.
- Date, time, number, and currency formats.
- Reading direction.
- Cultural meanings of icons and colours.
- Legal and regional requirements.

## Design Implication

Design should leave room for text expansion and avoid assumptions that only fit one language or culture.
