# Aesthetics & Colour

Aesthetics and colour affect first impressions, readability, emotional response, and perceived trust. They also support structure and meaning.

## Colour Uses

- Highlight priority.
- Indicate status.
- Group related items.
- Communicate warnings or success.
- Support brand or tone.

## Design Implication

Colour should not be only way to communicate meaning. Use labels, icons, patterns, or position for accessibility.
