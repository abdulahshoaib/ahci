# White Space

White space is empty space between interface elements. It improves readability, grouping, and focus.

## Uses

- Separate sections.
- Emphasize important content.
- Reduce clutter.
- Improve scanning.
- Support touch targets and interaction comfort.

## Design Implication

White space is functional. Too little creates clutter; too much can hide relationships or waste space.
