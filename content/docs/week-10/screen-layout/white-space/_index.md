# White Space

White space is the empty space between interface elements. It is not wasted space; it is an important design tool that improves readability, organization, and visual clarity.

White space helps users understand the structure of a screen by separating related and unrelated elements.

When viewing a screen, users often notice the spaces and gaps between items as much as the items themselves. These gaps help reveal the overall structure of the interface.

White space is used to:

- Separate groups of information
- Organize content into meaningful sections
- Reduce visual clutter
- Improve readability
- Highlight important information

## Space to Separate

White space can create clear boundaries between different groups of information, making the interface easier to understand.

## Space to Structure

White space helps reveal the layout and organization of a screen, allowing users to quickly identify related content and functions.

## Space to Highlight

Extra space around an element naturally draws attention to it, making important information or actions more noticeable.

## Benefits of White Space

- Improves visual clarity
- Reduces cognitive load
- Makes content easier to scan
- Supports grouping and organization
- Draws attention to important elements

Effective use of white space makes an interface feel cleaner, more organized, and easier to use without adding any extra visual elements.
