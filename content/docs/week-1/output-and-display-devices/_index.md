# Output and Display Devices

Output devices present information from the computer to the user. Displays are the most common output devices, but output can also be auditory, tactile, or physical.

## Common Output Devices

- Monitors and laptop screens
- Mobile displays
- Projectors
- Speakers and headphones
- Printers
- Haptic actuators
- VR and AR headsets

## HCI Considerations

Useful output must be perceivable, understandable, timely, and appropriate for the task. Designers must consider screen size, contrast, resolution, viewing distance, sound environment, and accessibility needs.
