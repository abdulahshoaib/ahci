# Observation & Ethnography

Observation studies users in real or realistic settings. Ethnography is deeper, longer-term study of people, practices, tools, culture, and context.

## Observation

- Shows real behavior.
- Reveals workarounds and hidden steps.
- Captures context users may forget to mention.

## Ethnography

- Studies users in natural environment.
- Focuses on social and cultural context.
- Useful for complex workplaces and communities.

## Design Implication

Observation helps uncover mismatch between official process and actual practice.
