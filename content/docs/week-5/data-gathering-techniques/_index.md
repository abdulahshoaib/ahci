# Data Gathering Techniques

Data gathering techniques help designers collect evidence about users, tasks, context, needs, and problems. Method choice depends on question being asked and stage of design.

## Common Techniques

- Interviews.
- Questionnaires.
- Observation.
- Ethnography.
- Contextual inquiry.
- Brainstorming.

## Design Implication

Use multiple techniques when possible. Interviews reveal what people say; observation reveals what people actually do.
