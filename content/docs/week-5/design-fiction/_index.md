# Design Fiction

Design fiction uses imagined future products, services, or situations to explore possible impacts of technology. It helps designers think beyond immediate features.

## Purpose

- Explore future use contexts.
- Question assumptions.
- Surface ethical and social effects.
- Communicate speculative design ideas.

## Design Implication

Design fiction is useful when technology may change behavior, culture, privacy, work, or relationships.
