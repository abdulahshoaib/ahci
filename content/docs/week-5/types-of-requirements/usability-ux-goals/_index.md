# Usability & UX Goals

Usability goals define how well users should complete tasks. UX goals describe desired qualities of whole experience.

## Usability Goals

- Effectiveness.
- Efficiency.
- Safety.
- Learnability.
- Memorability.
- Accessibility.

## UX Goals

- Trustworthy.
- Satisfying.
- Engaging.
- Calm.
- Enjoyable.
- Professional.

## Design Implication

Goals should be specific enough to evaluate. "Easy to use" is weak; "new users complete checkout without help in under three minutes" is stronger.
