# Discovering Requirements

Discovering requirements means finding out what system must support before design and implementation. In HCI, requirements come from users, tasks, context, data, usability goals, and stakeholder constraints.

## Purpose

- Understand real user goals.
- Identify current problems and opportunities.
- Clarify tasks and workflows.
- Capture environmental and technical constraints.
- Define success criteria for design and evaluation.

## Design Implication

Requirements discovery reduces guesswork. Good design starts with evidence about people, work, and context.
