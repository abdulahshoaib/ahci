# Dark Patterns in UI/UX

Dark patterns are interface designs that manipulate or deceive users into choices they may not intend.

## Examples

- Hidden costs.
- Forced continuity.
- Hard cancellation.
- Trick questions.
- Confirmshaming.
- Sneaking items into cart.

## Design Implication

Dark patterns may raise short-term metrics but damage trust, ethics, and long-term user relationships.
