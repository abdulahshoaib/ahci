# Bad Website Design Examples

Bad website design makes users work harder than necessary. Problems often appear in navigation, layout, readability, responsiveness, accessibility, and trust.

## Signs

- Users cannot find key content.
- Pages feel cluttered or confusing.
- Text is hard to read.
- Interface breaks on mobile.
- Important actions are hidden.
- Errors are hard to recover from.

## Design Implication

Bad examples are useful because they reveal what to avoid and how design choices affect real user behavior.
