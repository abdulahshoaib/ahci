# UX Goals

UX goals describe qualities of experience beyond task completion.

## Examples

- Satisfying.
- Trustworthy.
- Enjoyable.
- Calm.
- Engaging.
- Professional.
- Empowering.

## Design Implication

UX goals should match domain. Banking may need trust and clarity; game may need challenge and excitement.
