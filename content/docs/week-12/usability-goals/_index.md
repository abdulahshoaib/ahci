# Usability Goals

Usability goals define how well users should complete tasks.

## Common Goals

- Effectiveness.
- Efficiency.
- Safety.
- Utility.
- Learnability.
- Memorability.
- Accessibility.

## Design Implication

Good goals are measurable. Example: "90% of new users complete registration without help."
