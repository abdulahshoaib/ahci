# Measuring Usability and UX

Measuring usability and UX turns design goals into evidence.

## Usability Measures

- Completion rate.
- Time on task.
- Error rate.
- Help requests.
- Learnability over attempts.

## UX Measures

- Satisfaction ratings.
- Trust ratings.
- Perceived effort.
- Preference.
- Qualitative feedback.

## Design Implication

Measurement should connect to task and goal. Random metrics can mislead design decisions.
