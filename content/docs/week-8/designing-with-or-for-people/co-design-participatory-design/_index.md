# Co-design and Participatory Design

Co-design and participatory design involve users, stakeholders, or community members as active partners in design. They help ensure systems reflect real needs, values, and contexts.

## Practices

- Workshops.
- Sketching sessions.
- Prototype critique.
- Collaborative mapping.
- Shared prioritization.

## Design Implication

Participants are not only data sources. They help define problems and shape solutions.
