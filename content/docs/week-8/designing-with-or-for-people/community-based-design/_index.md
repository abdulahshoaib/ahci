# Community-Based Design

Community-based design works with groups affected by a system, especially when design decisions influence shared resources, culture, access, or local practices.

## Key Concerns

- Community goals.
- Trust and representation.
- Local knowledge.
- Long-term effects.
- Ethical participation.

## Design Implication

Designers must avoid extracting input without benefit. Community design requires respect, accountability, and shared decision making.
