# Designing with or for People

Designing for people means designers study users and create solutions on their behalf. Designing with people means users and stakeholders actively participate in shaping design decisions.

## Difference

- **For people:** designers interpret user needs and make design choices.
- **With people:** users contribute knowledge, priorities, ideas, and evaluation.

## Design Implication

More participation can improve relevance, ownership, and trust, but it requires time, facilitation, and careful handling of power differences.
