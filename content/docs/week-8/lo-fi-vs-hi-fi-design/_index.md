# Lo-fi vs Hi-fi Design

Lo-fi and hi-fi design differ in detail, effort, realism, and evaluation purpose.

## Lo-fi

- Fast and cheap.
- Easy to change.
- Good for early concepts and flows.
- Less useful for fine visual or interactive detail.

## Hi-fi

- More realistic.
- Better for detailed interaction testing.
- More convincing for stakeholders.
- More expensive and harder to change.

## Design Implication

Start low fidelity when uncertainty is high. Move high fidelity when direction is stable.
