# Low-Fidelity Prototyping

Low-fidelity prototypes are quick, rough representations of design ideas. They focus on concepts, structure, and flow rather than visual polish or full functionality.

## Examples

- Paper sketches.
- Storyboards.
- Index cards.
- Click-through wireframes.
- Wizard-of-Oz simulations.

## Design Implication

Low-fidelity prototypes invite critique because they look unfinished. This helps teams change direction early.
