# Sketching Techniques

Sketching techniques help designers create and compare many rough ideas quickly. Sketches are disposable tools for thinking, not finished artwork.

## Good Sketching Practice

- Draw many alternatives.
- Keep detail low at first.
- Annotate important behavior.
- Use sketches to discuss, not decorate.
- Iterate based on feedback.

## Design Implication

Sketching supports divergent thinking before team converges on one design direction.
