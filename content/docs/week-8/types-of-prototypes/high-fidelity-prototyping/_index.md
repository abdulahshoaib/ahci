# High-Fidelity Prototyping

High-fidelity prototypes look and behave close to final product. They may include realistic visuals, content, interactions, and sometimes working code.

## Strengths

- Useful for testing detailed interactions.
- Helps stakeholders imagine final product.
- Supports visual and interaction refinement.
- Can reveal implementation constraints.

## Weaknesses

- More expensive to build.
- May discourage major changes.
- Users may judge polish instead of concept.
